How to use the BTime cube timer

BTime is a free online speedcubing timer for desktop and mobile practice. It combines puzzle scrambles and drawings with solve history, inspection, and averages. You can start practicing without creating an account.

In this guide
  • Time a solve
  • Choose a puzzle and scramble
  • Understand your averages
  • Review solves and penalties
  • Install BTime and practice offline
  • Where your history is saved

Time a solve on desktop or phone

  1. Open the BTime timer, select your puzzle, and scramble it using the displayed moves.
  2. On desktop, hold Space until the timer is ready, then release to start. Press a key to stop and save your solve.
  3. On a phone, hold and release the timer surface to start. Tap again to stop.

In settings, enable 15s inspection if you want an inspection countdown before your solve. With inspection enabled, your first Space press or tap starts inspection; the next hold and release starts the timer.

Hold delay requires a 300 ms hold before starting. With it off, BTime still requires a short 100 ms press to help prevent accidental starts. Hide time while solving keeps your running time out of view until you finish.

Enter a time manually

Enable Manual time entry, type your result in the timer, and press Enter. Both 12.34 and 1234 record 12.34 seconds. Use 1:20.00 for one minute and twenty seconds, or 12.34+2 to record 12.34 seconds with a two-second penalty.

Puzzle scrambles and drawings

Choose 2x2, 3x3, 3x3 one-handed, 4x4, 5x5, 6x6, 7x7, Clock, Megaminx, Pyraminx, Skewb, or Square-1 from the puzzle selector. Use the refresh button for a new scramble. The drawing shows the scrambled puzzle so you can check your moves.

BTime keeps each event's history and averages separate. The 3x3 one-handed event uses 3x3 scrambles with its own history. Scrambles are generated locally using TNoodle; some events take longer to initialize on their first use.

Ao5, Ao12, and longer averages

Ao5 is the average of your latest five solves after removing the fastest and slowest result. Ao12 uses your latest twelve solves and also removes one result at each end. Averages appear once you have enough solves for the selected event.

For longer sessions, BTime also shows Ao25, Ao50, and Ao100. These remove the fastest and slowest 5% of results, rounded up at each end. That means two results at each end for Ao25, three for Ao50, and five for Ao100.

A +2 penalty counts toward the result before averaging. A DNF can be removed among the worst results, but any DNF left in the counted results makes the average DNF. Mean includes all non-DNF solves for the current event.

Select an average to see and copy its breakdown. Parentheses mark excluded results. Best single opens your fastest solve; Best Ao5 opens your best five-solve average.

Review solve history and apply penalties

Select a saved solve to inspect its time, scramble, and drawing. Use +2 to add a two-second penalty, DNF for a solve you did not finish, or Revert to remove a penalty. You can also copy the scramble or delete the solve.

On desktop, Ctrl+1 reverts a penalty, Ctrl+2 applies +2, and Ctrl+3 marks a DNF. These act on the selected solve while its details are open, or the most recent solve otherwise. Alt+Z asks to delete that solve; Alt+D asks to clear the current event's history.

The history list shows 50 records per page. Use Newer and Older to browse longer sessions.

Install BTime and use it offline

Visit BTime while connected and wait for Settings > BTime app > Offline ready. Once setup finishes, the timer and scramble generators work without an internet connection.

Choose Install BTime when your browser offers it. On iPhone or iPad, use Share > Add to Home Screen. Installation availability depends on your browser; you can also use BTime in a regular tab.

When an update is ready, BTime displays Update available, tap to refresh. Accept it between solves. App updates preserve your saved history and preferences.

Where are my times saved?

Your history stays in your current browser profile on this device. BTime uses IndexedDB for solve history and localStorage for preferences, with a basic history fallback when IndexedDB is unavailable. There is no fixed solve-count limit; available browser storage is the practical limit.

History does not automatically sync between devices or browsers. Clearing site data removes saved times and settings. Private browsing may remove them when the private session closes.
